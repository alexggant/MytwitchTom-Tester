# Twitch Space 2.3: what's new

*Formerly MytwitchTom. Same extension, new name.*

## New name
- MytwitchTom is now **Twitch Space**. Your old card on the extensions page still says MytwitchTom until you install 2.3.
- Ask the Streamer still works between 2.2 and 2.3.

## Turn features on or off
- Click the **Twitch Space icon** in your browser's toolbar (the upside-down Tom). Pin it from the puzzle-piece menu if you don't see it.
- Each bigger feature has a switch. Switch one off, click **Refresh Twitch**, and it doesn't run at all.

## Ask the Streamer: connections
- It now only works between two people who have **both added each other**. Two strangers with the extension can't send each other questions.
- On a channel you haven't connected with, the **Ask** button is greyed out. Click it, then **Add to your connections**.
- On your own channel, **Questions** shows your whole list. Search to add people; **×** disconnects them.
- The Ask / Questions button and the question cards only show where the chat is on screen (the stream or chat view), never on a channel's Home tab. A question that arrives while you're on Home waits there until you open the chat.
- On your own channel, **Questions** sits in the header next to the Edit pencil.

## Emote picker
- Twitch's new picker dropped the column of channel pictures on the right. Twitch Space puts it back: click a channel to jump to its emotes; right-click to add it to Frequent Channels.
- Clicking a channel in **Frequent Channels** jumps straight to its emotes again.

## Watch streaks
- Hovering the flame shows just the **target** (minutes a watch needs to count, and how it learned that) and **minutes watched**.
- When the countdown runs out, the flame steps aside for a moment so Twitch's own streak pop-up can show, then comes back with the ✓.
- **Streak Saver** now runs out of sight: its window is minimized and muted. When Twitch confirms it, a pop-up on your Twitch tab says **"Streak saved with (channel)"**.

## Chat
- Your name and highlight words get their box the moment a message appears, so the text no longer jumps sideways.

## Channel pages
- An offline channel's Home page no longer autoplays its recent stream with sound (it starts muted; live streams aren't affected).
- Clicking a **pinned channel** that's offline opens its **Home** tab, not the chat view. Live channels still open on the stream.

## Updating from an older version
1. Go to `brave://extensions` or `chrome://extensions`.
2. Click **Remove** on the old card (MytwitchTom or Twitch Space) and confirm.
3. Download **TwitchSpace-2.3.zip**, unzip it, click **Load unpacked**, and pick the **Twitch Space** folder.
4. Refresh Twitch.

Removing the old one clears its saved phrases, notes, pins and markers, so jot down anything you want to keep first.
